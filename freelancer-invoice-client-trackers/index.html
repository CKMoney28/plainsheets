Free invoice & client tracker templates for freelancers

17 free Excel and Google Sheets templates for billing clients and keeping track of who owes you. We opened every link on November 5, 2026 and noted any catch, such as an email gate, a "pay what you want" box, or a software upsell. We don't sell any of these. Some publishers are software companies that use free templates to bring in customers, and we say so where that applies.

Quick picks

  • Just want to know who hasn't paid: InvoicesTracker or the SheetFor tracker.
  • Clients, projects and invoices in one file: the yoshibuilds Client & Invoice Tracker.
  • Need the invoice document itself: Vertex42 or Microsoft Create.
  • Follow-up reminders: the OnePageCRM Client Tracker.
