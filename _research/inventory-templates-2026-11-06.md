# PlainSheets research: Inventory / stock templates for Etsy + Shopify SMBs
Checked: 2026-11-06 (America/New_York). Only pages opened via WebFetch/WebSearch; no invented features/URLs.

## Quick picks (4 buckets)

1. **Just need low-stock / reorder alerts** — Sheetorial STOCKORA; Quiriz Inventory & Reorder Tracker; Smartsheet Google Sheets Inventory Template.
2. **Etsy handmade / multi-variation stock + fees** — Findig Free Etsy Inventory Spreadsheet; Craftybase Free Etsy Inventory Spreadsheet.
3. **COGS + profit (makers or product sellers)** — Stocksmith Free Inventory & COGS; Craftybase (materials → manufactures → COGS); Qoblex workbook (retail COGS/dashboard).
4. **Simple starter / catalog + stock** — Vertex42 Stock Inventory Control; Spreadsheet Daddy Small Business Inventory; Microsoft Create inventories hub.
