# Sales trackers, daily sales reports & forecasts research
Checked 2026-11-24 (America/New_York) via WebSearch/curl; workbooks opened with openpyxl to confirm tabs/formulas/dropdowns, and recalculated in LibreOffice headless to check for formula errors (none besides intentional chart #N/A gaps in ExcelChamps). Files in /workspace/niche-sites/_dl/sales.
Listed 10 in 4 groups:
- Daily sales reports: FileConvy daily sales report (~20 bill lines, payment dropdown, totals by method, till over/short; one tab per day; xlsx+PDF; "free for personal and commercial use"), Fuze Store daily sales report (31 day rows, cash/e-wallet/QR/card/bank, net sales, expected cash = float + cash only, variance; gross typed not summed; Philippines focus), The Owner's Notebook daily sales flash (restaurant: net sales, guests, labor $, voids -> avg check, labor %, void %, vs same day last week, OVER flag vs 30% target; ~1 month rows).
- Sales trackers & targets: Groundwork Metrics simple sales tracker (1,000-row log, 200 items, slow mover = in stock & 90 days no sale, low stock <=3, dashboard + 2 charts; uses MAXIFS -> Excel 2019+; site sells Shopify profit reports), CtrlSheet sales tracker (100 orders, product/channel dropdowns, gross profit per product, By month AOV; xlsx + Google Sheets copy; 2 downloads/24h limit; free personal+commercial), SimplifyLearn sales tracker (300-row log, monthly targets vs actual, status Target met/Close/Below, by salesperson incl. unpaid; unpaid excludes Partly paid; no resale).
- Forecasts: Vertex42 3-year sales forecast (units x price, COGS, margin, gross profit, 7 charts; sample start 2022; Excel only; private-use license; xlsx from /Files/exclusive/), Smartsheet retail sales forecast (footfall -> customers -> sales -> revenue, 5 years, example + blank; Google Sheets copy export 200; Smartsheet banner).
- Commission: Office Templates Online tiered commission calculator (Settings tiers 5/8/10/12%, sales log w/ refunds, monthly calc rows typed per rep, Pending/Paid, dashboard; Excel tables; whole month at one tier; "this month" = TODAY so sample Apr-Sep 2026 shows $0), Landing commission calculator (10 reps, quarter quota, attainment bands 2/4/6/8%, 10% accelerator above quota, £ labels, no sales log; band rate applies to all sales up to quota).
Direct .xlsx (valid OOXML zip) confirmed for all 10; Google Sheets exports for CtrlSheet and Smartsheet retail returned 200.
