# Small business budget & startup cost templates research
Checked 2026-11-27 (America/New_York) via WebSearch/curl; every listed page returned 200 and every listed file downloaded without an email or account (Google Sheets links: /copy returns 302 to Google sign-in to make a copy, /export?format=xlsx 200). Workbooks opened with openpyxl (tabs/formulas/dropdowns/protection, no VBA in any) and recalculated in LibreOffice headless (no formula errors in any listed file). Spot tests with typed numbers for Business Tools Lab, Smartsheet startup + professional budgets and business.com (in test/). Files in /workspace/niche-sites/_dl/budget (recalc/ = recalculated copies). Builder: /workspace/niche-sites/scripts-notes/build_budget_page.py.
Why this category: no existing page covers an annual operating budget / budget vs actual or startup costs (cash flow page lists Glassjar budget + cash flow workbook; P&L page lists income statements). Real demand ("small business budget template", "budget vs actual template", "startup costs template").
Listed 10 in 3 groups:
- Budget vs actual: Bookkeeping Sheets small business budget (Setup/Budget/Actuals/Variance/Report, 50 lines, 5 groups, fiscal-year start month, report-through month, $ and % review thresholds -> Favorable/Unfavorable flags, YTD + full-year forecast = YTD actual + remaining budget, top-5 unfavorable, chart; sample Riverside Coffee Co.; same site already on AP + daily cash pages), Business Tools Lab small business budget (Monthly Budget 22 fixed categories x 12 months budget/actual/variance, Dashboard annual budget/actual/variance + monthly net; blank; monthly variance is "good = positive" (revenue actual-budget, expenses budget-actual) but Dashboard variance is actual-budget for every line, so expenses over budget show positive there; adding categories needs dashboard formula edits; Setup sheet is reference only), Vertex42 business budget (Services + Goods sheets, one year Actual/Budget/Difference, % of sales in COGS version; Difference = actual - budget for all lines; header says 2022; private use license), Smartsheet annual business budget (monthly income + expense lines with yearly Budget/Actual/Over-Under for expenses only; income has no budget column; summary chart; "create in Smartsheet" banner; reference-only disclaimer), business.gov.au budget (Australian gov; July-June months; budget/actual per month, 11 income + 14 expense lines, summary differences = budget - actual; assumes figures include GST; blank).
- Monthly plan from drivers: Vertex42 12-month business budget (Excel + Google Sheets copy; Services/Goods; monthly plan only, no actual columns; COGS detail per product with beginning/ending inventory), Covirage driver-based budget (Assumptions burden rate, products units x price x COGS%, 12-month phasing weights; People headcount x salary x (1+burden) from start month; Costs flat/seasonal; Summary by quarter + month with 5 checks = 0; BvA one month at a time, no YTD; sample $4.23M company, FY2027; Covirage sells an analysis tool).
- Startup costs: Vertex42 business startup costs (funding owners/loans/grants est vs actual; ~30 one-time costs + ~26 monthly costs x months until profitable; surplus/deficit; Restaurant sheet; tips column; private use; AI version needs Excel 2019+), BusinessPlanDownload startup costs calculator (one sheet: 12 one-time + 9 monthly, months of reserve, owner investment, funding gap = MAX(0,...); example $95,700 need / $75,700 gap; no budget vs actual; site has a business plan builder), Smartsheet startup budget (funding investors/loans/other + expenses admin/location/marketing/pre-launch labor/other, budget vs actual + over/under, date-due column; Excel + Google Sheets; sign of over/under differs between funding (actual-budget) and costs (budget-actual)).
Left out (11): Sheetgo budget vs actual (actual income/expense formulas use two lower bounds, so each month's actual includes every later month: Jan Landing Page actual 40,100 = whole year), Enerpize budget vs actual (email required for the copy), business.com small business budget (Total Expenses SUM starts below Payroll so payroll is left out; Overview annual sales budget points at Other Revenue total), Smartsheet professional business budget (Actual sheet rows sit two rows above Budget sheet rows; line variances compare different items though totals match), Smartsheet 12-month business budget (EBIT/Other rows typed, not calculated; sample has negative COGS), AllBizDoc annual budget planner (works, but household categories: groceries, childcare, salary), SCORE startup expenses (403 to our checker, file not verified), NH SBDC estimated start-up costs (connection failed), WorkspaceTemplates small business budget / budget vs actual / business goals tracker (download goes through a bot-check token; couldn't get the file), QuickBooks business budget (connection refused to our checker), Smarfle small business budget (link opened their job tracker instead).
