Monthly or 13-week?

A 12-month forecast is good for spotting seasonal dips and planning a loan or a big purchase. A 13-week weekly forecast is better when cash is tight now, because it shows the exact week a payroll, tax bill or slow-paying customer pushes you low, which a monthly view averages away. In either one, enter money when it actually hits or leaves the bank, not when you send the invoice.
