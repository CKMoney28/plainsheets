What a mileage log needs

IRS Publication 463 asks for the date, the destination, the business purpose and the miles for each business trip, plus your total miles for the year. Write trips down at or near the time; updating weekly is generally treated as timely, while rebuilding a year from memory in April often doesn't hold up. Note your odometer on January 1 and December 31, and leave out your regular commute. For 2026 the standard business rate changed mid-year (72.5 cents a mile through June, 76 cents from July 1), so pick a template that applies the rate by trip date. Ask a tax professional about your own situation.
