Leave trackers, PTO accrual, onboarding and training records: which one do you need?

A leave tracker records who took time off and when, against a fixed yearly allowance. A PTO accrual tracker is for policies where people earn time off as they work, usually a set number of hours each pay period, often with a maximum balance and a limit on what carries into next year. Write your policy down first and track in either days or hours, never both. An onboarding checklist makes sure the paperwork, equipment, logins and check-ins happen for every new hire; in the US that includes the W-4 and Form I-9, which has to be completed within three business days of the start date. An employee database is your one list of who works for you, and a training tracker shows who has done which training and when certificates expire. These sheets keep records; they aren't legal advice, and leave, payout and record-keeping rules vary by state, so check yours. Employee leave tracker (monthly calendar)

by Vertex42
ExcelFree

A calendar sheet for each month and a year-to-date summary for up to 30 employees. Type or pick a letter code on each day (V vacation, S sick, P personal, D disability, O other paid, U unpaid; rename them to suit you), use HV or VH for a half day, and each month and the year add up the days by type for every person. Change the year once and all 12 calendars update. Good when you mostly want to see who is off when.

Catch: No sign-up; direct .xlsx, Excel only (2013 or later). Private-use license. It counts days, not hours, and doesn't work out balances or accrual; the year summary also counts leave you've booked for later in the year. The sample is set to 2023. For accrual by pay period, see LeaveCalc below.

PTO tracker with accrual, cap and carryover

by LeaveCalc
ExcelGoogle SheetsFree

Built the way US payroll handles PTO: pick weekly, biweekly, semi-monthly or monthly pay, the hours earned each pay period, a maximum balance (cap) and how many hours carry into next year. Log time off by the hour and it shows each person's balance at the end of every pay period, their balance today and on December 31, hours not earned because they hit the cap and hours they'll lose at year end. Each person can have their own rate, cap or hire date. Up to 30 people.

Catch: No sign-up; direct .xlsx (87 KB, 7 sheets). For Google Sheets you upload it. It's set up for 2027, so for this year change the tracker year and first pay date on Setup. Hours only; it converts to days using your workday length. The site also promotes its own leave-tracking tools; you don't need them.

Leave tracker with a request log

by RedStone HR
ExcelFree

List each person with their yearly allowance and days carried over, then log every request once: employee, leave type and status from dropdowns, first and last day, and a half-day option. It counts working days Monday to Friday, skips the public holidays you list, and shows what each person has taken, what's requested and still pending, what's left, and approved sick days. Room for 50 employees and 500 requests.

Catch: No sign-up; direct .xlsx. A fixed yearly allowance, not accrual. Only approved rows of the first leave type count as taken, and "remaining" doesn't subtract pending requests. Monday to Friday only, so it won't suit weekend shift workers as is. Save a copy and clear the log each new leave year. RedStone sells leave software.

New hire onboarding checklist

by Smartsheet
ExcelGoogle SheetsWordFree

A one-page checklist per new hire: name, role, start date and manager at the top, then task tables for preboarding, the first day, the first week and 30, 60 and 90 days, each with who owns it, a due date, a status dropdown and notes. Onboarding checklist with automatic due dates

by SimplifyLearn
ExcelFree

26 ready-made tasks from before day 1 to the month-3 review. Enter the start date once and every task gets its due date (tasks before day 1 use negative days). Pick the owner (HR, IT, manager, buddy, employee or finance), mark each task Yes, No or N/A, and the top shows overall progress, progress for each phase, tasks done and how many are overdue. Late tasks turn red and finished ones green.

Catch: No sign-up; direct .xlsx (our first try was refused for too many requests; a retry a few seconds later worked). For Google Sheets you import it. Tasks marked N/A still count against the progress percentage. Free for personal and business use, not for resale. Copy the sheet for each new hire.

Onboarding checklist to day 90

by flair
ExcelFree

26 tasks in six phases: before day one, day one, week one, the first 30 days, days 31 to 60 and days 61 to 90. Each task has an owner, a number of days from the start date and a due date worked out for you; set the status to open, in progress, done or not applicable. The progress percentage leaves out tasks you mark not applicable, and overdue tasks turn red.

Catch: No sign-up; direct .xlsx, Excel only (upload it to use in Google Sheets). The start date cell is a formula (today plus 21 days), so type the real date over it. One new hire per sheet. flair sells HR software; the checklist doesn't need it.

Employee record and database

by KadrHR
ExcelFree

One row per person with 16 columns: employee ID, name, job title, department, manager, employment type and status (both dropdowns), start and end date, work email, phone, location, annual leave allowance, emergency contact and notes. Room for 200 people. Leavers stay in the list marked Left and turn grey instead of being deleted, and a Read Me sheet covers which details to keep out of a shared file.

Employee training tracker with renewal dates

by Ethica
ExcelFree

One row per person, per training or policy, per round: what it was, the version or certificate number, the due date, when they finished, where the signed form or certificate is kept, and how many months until it repeats. It works out the next due date and flags each row overdue, due soon, complete or renewal overdue. A filled-in example uses US rules, such as forklift evaluations every three years, and a how-to tab lists common renewal intervals.

Catch: No email; direct .xlsx with 300 rows. For Google Sheets you upload it. You decide which training each role needs; the sheet only tracks dates. Status uses today's date, so it changes as time passes. Ethica sells training software ($49 a month per 50 employees); the tracker doesn't need it.

Training plan with due dates and a summary

by Excel TV
ExcelGoogle SheetsFree

List your employees with their start dates and your courses with how many days new hires get to finish each one. On the training log, pick the employee and course; it fills in the name, department and due date (start date plus the course window) and flags anything overdue against an "as of" date you set. Training matrix with expiry warnings

by FieldClear
ExcelFree

A grid with one row per worker and a column for each certificate or course (first aid, manual handling, fire marshal, working at height and company induction to start). Enter each expiry date, or N/A where it doesn't apply, and it shows current, expiring soon (within 60 days) or expired, plus days left. A second sheet notes which requirements each role needs. Suits crews where everyone holds tickets that lapse.

Catch: No email; direct .xlsx, free including commercial use. UK wording and examples. The role requirements sheet is a reference only; it doesn't check the grid for you. Adding a course means copying three columns. The last tab advertises FieldClear's online version (14-day free trial).
