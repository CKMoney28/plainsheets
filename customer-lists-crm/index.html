List, lead tracker or CRM: which do you need?

A customer list is a phone book: names, contact details and maybe a category. A lead tracker adds a status and a next follow-up date, which matters most if you win work by quoting and chasing. A spreadsheet CRM adds deals with values and stages, so you can see what's likely to close and how often you win. Start with the smallest one that covers your problem. The column that does the most work in all of them is the next follow-up date, so fill it in every time you talk to someone. A spreadsheet starts to strain when two or more people edit it at once, or when follow-ups only happen if someone remembers to open the file. Customer lists hold personal information, so keep the file private and don't paste it into a quote or invoice you send out.

Free CRM template

by thefrontkit
ExcelSheetsFree

Five tabs: Start Here, Lists, Contacts, Deals and Dashboard. Contacts carry status, owner, last-contacted and next follow-up dates. Each deal gets a stage from New to Negotiation, and the matching probability and weighted value fill in for you. The dashboard adds up open pipeline, weighted pipeline, all-time won and win rate. Stages, probabilities and team names are edited on the Lists tab.

Catch: Direct .xlsx with no email. The file says it's free for personal and commercial use. The publisher sells Next.js app builds and mentions that on the page.

CRM template

by Vertex42
ExcelSheetsFree

A main Customers table with estimated sale, last contact, next action, next contact, lead status and lead source. Lead tracker for Excel

by SoftoTech
ExcelSheetsFree

One row per lead with last contact, a next follow-up date it works out for you, days since contact and a flag that turns the row red with “Follow up now” once you're late. Won and lost leads drop out of the overdue list. The Summary tab shows leads and value by status and source, win rate and follow-ups due, with a chart. The sample rows suit real estate, but the lead types and sources can be changed.

Catch: Direct .xlsx with no email. You type today's date into the Summary sheet yourself, so the overdue flags don't update until you do. We didn't spot an upsell on the page.
