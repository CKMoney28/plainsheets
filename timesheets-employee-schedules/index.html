What a US timesheet should capture

Under the Fair Labor Standards Act, employers keep a record of hours worked each workday and total hours each workweek for non-exempt employees, and the Department of Labor's Fact Sheet 21 says payroll records are generally kept for at least three years and time cards for two. Overtime for non-exempt staff is usually owed at 1.5 times the regular rate for hours over 40 in a workweek, figured week by week, so a biweekly sheet should never average two weeks together. Many states add their own rules on daily overtime and breaks. Check your state's rules or ask a payroll professional; these templates are record-keeping tools, not legal or payroll advice.

Weekly, biweekly & monthly timesheets

by Chronotek
ExcelSheetsPDFFree

Enter date, time in, time out and unpaid break minutes; it totals decimal hours, splits overtime at 40 hours per workweek (biweekly weeks are never averaged), works out gross pay at 1.5x and handles overnight shifts. There's also a blank editable version and a printable PDF.

Catch: Chronotek sells time-tracking software. Direct .xlsx downloads, no sign-up or email. For Sheets, upload to Drive and use File, Save as Google Sheets.
