Sales reports, trackers, forecasts and commission: which one do you need?

A daily sales report is the end-of-day check: what you took, how customers paid, and whether the cash in the drawer matches. A few minutes at closing catches a mistake the same day, while it can still be traced. A sales tracker is the running log behind it, one row per sale or per day, so you can see which products, channels and days carry the business; it only works if the same product names are typed the same way every time. A sales forecast is a planning guess, not a promise: start from last year's actual months, change only what you have a reason to change, and compare it with what really happened each month. A commission calculator should copy your written commission plan exactly, including how refunds are handled. These sheets record and add up; they aren't a POS or accounting software. Daily sales report with till check

by FileConvy
ExcelPDFFree

One sheet for one day at the counter: about 20 lines for bill number, customer, item, quantity and rate, with a payment dropdown (Cash, Card, Bank transfer, Online / wallet or Credit). It totals the day's sales by payment method, then runs a till check: opening cash plus cash sales minus cash expenses gives the cash you should have, and when you type in what you counted it shows the amount over or short. Signature lines for the cashier and the manager.

Catch: No sign-up; direct .xlsx, a printable PDF, and an optional edit-in-browser version on the same page. It's one tab per day with no monthly roll-up, so you copy the tab each day. Sales marked Credit count in the day's total but aren't tracked anywhere after that. Clear the [Item] and [Name] placeholders first; the date hint is DD/MM/YYYY.

Daily sales report: a whole month on one sheet

by Fuze Store
ExcelFree

31 numbered day rows. For each day you enter the opening float, takings by payment type (cash, e-wallet, QR, card and bank transfer), gross sales, discounts, refunds and the cash you counted at close; it works out net sales, the cash that should be in the drawer and the variance. A totals row adds up the month, and a short How to use tab explains every column.

Catch: No sign-up or email; direct .xlsx. It was written for small shops and food stalls in the Philippines, so rename the e-wallet and QR columns to match how your customers pay. Gross sales is typed in, not added up from the payment columns, so check the two agree. Expected cash is the float plus cash sales only; if you pay small expenses out of the drawer, the sheet's own notes tell you to subtract them yourself.

Daily sales flash report for restaurants and cafes

by The Owner's Notebook
ExcelFree

One row per day, copied from your POS end-of-day report: net sales, guest count, labor dollars, and voids and comps. It works out the average check, labor as a percentage of sales, void percentage and the change against the same day last week, and flags OVER on any day labor runs above your target (30% in the sample). Totals at the bottom show the period's sales, overall labor % and average check. Room for about a month of days.

Catch: No sign-up; an 8 KB direct download that goes with the site's guide to setting up daily sales reports in a POS. It doesn't add up tickets; you copy totals from your POS and labor figures from payroll or scheduling. The "vs last week" column stays blank for the first seven days. A sample week to clear first.

Simple sales tracker with top sellers and slow movers

by Groundwork Metrics
ExcelFree

A Sales Log with room for 1,000 sales (date, item, category, channel, quantity, price; weekday and month fill in). The Items tab, with room for 200 items and their stock on hand, works out units sold, revenue, last sale date and days since, and flags slow movers (in stock but no sale in 90 days) and low stock (3 or fewer). The Dashboard shows total sales, units, average sale, best and slowest weekday, sales by day of week, channel and month, your top five sellers and slow movers, with two charts. No macros.

Catch: No sign-up; the download page starts the .xlsx. It needs Excel 2019 or newer (it uses MAXIFS); for Google Sheets you upload it to Drive. Item names on the Sales Log must match the Items tab exactly. Comes filled with a made-up gift shop to delete. The site sells Shopify profit reports and offers a free dashboard setup; you don't need either to use the file.

by CtrlSheet

Sales tracker with monthly targets vs actual

by SimplifyLearn
ExcelFree

A sales log with 300 rows: date, invoice number, customer, product or service, salesperson and payment status (Paid, Partly paid or Unpaid) from dropdowns, quantity and unit price. On the Summary sheet you type the year and a target for each month; it shows actual sales, the gap, % of target, number of sales and a status (Target met, Close or Below), plus a by-salesperson table with sales count, total, unpaid amount and share.

Sample sales from a small design and print shop to clear. Free for personal and business use; the page asks you not to resell it.

Tiered sales commission calculator and tracker

by Office Templates Online
ExcelFree

Set your commission tiers on the Settings tab (the sample pays 5% from $0, 8% from $5,000, 10% from $10,000 and 12% from $20,000 of monthly net sales), plus your reps and currency. Log each sale with the rep from a dropdown and any refund; Commission Calculation totals each rep's net sales for the month, finds the tier, works out the commission and tracks Pending or Paid. The Dashboard shows commission payable, paid, the average rate and this month's top earner, with two charts. No macros.

Catch: No email; the download button appears after a short wait, and the page carries ads. You add a row for each rep and month on the Commission Calculation tab yourself. The whole month is paid at the single tier rate it reaches, not band by band, so check that matches your plan. Built with Excel tables for desktop Excel. "This month" follows today's date, so with the sample data (April to September 2026) it shows $0.

Quarterly commission calculator with quota accelerator

by Landing
ExcelFree

Ten rep rows: type each rep's quarter quota and actual sales, and it works out attainment, looks up the rate from your bands (the sample pays 2% below 50% of quota, 4% from 50%, 6% from 80% and 8% at quota), pays that rate on sales up to quota plus a 10% accelerator on everything above it, and adds a team total. Attainment turns red, amber or green. Rates live on a Settings tab, and the page also has an in-browser version.

Catch: No sign-up; direct .xlsx. The site sells all-in-one business software and has a contact form for it; you don't need either to use the file. Amounts are labeled in pounds (£), so change the currency format. There's no sales log: you type each rep's quarter total. The band rate applies to all sales up to quota, not just the slice inside each band, so check it against your commission plan.
